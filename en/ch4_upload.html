Chapter 4

Upload photos and videos

Get the photos and videos on your computer or phone into Immich. By the end of this chapter, you'll be able to send your memories to the server by drag and drop or with the Upload button, and you'll understand upload progress, duplicate detection and retrying after a failure.

Photos have to come in before you can organize them

The value of Immich starts with getting your photos into one place. Whether they come from a camera memory card, a folder on your computer or your phone, the timeline, albums and AI search only become useful once the photos are on the server. This chapter starts with the most direct method: uploading from the web.

Chapter task: upload a batch of photos from a browser, and make sense of the upload progress and the result. Automatic backup from your phone is Chapter 9; this chapter covers the basics of uploading from the web.

Two ways to upload, and what happens behind the scenes

MethodHowBest for
Drag and dropDrag files from a folder straight into the browser windowDropping many photos at once, or sorting through a camera memory card
Upload buttonClick Upload on the right of the top bar, then choose the filesWhen you are not used to drag and drop, or want to pick files precisely

During an upload, Immich does several things: it reads the photo's EXIF data (capture time, location, camera model), generates thumbnails, and runs duplicate detection. Once uploaded, a photo appears on the timeline by its capture time, not by its upload time.

Sorted by capture time: if a photo seems to have "jumped" to some date in the past, that is usually the capture time in its EXIF data, and it is normal.

Six steps to upload from the web

  1. Log in and stay on the Photos page

    Log in as in Chapter 2, and make sure you are on the main photos screen (/photos).

  2. Get the files ready

    On your computer, open the folder that holds the photos. You can select several files at once, or a whole folder.

  3. Drag them into the window, or click Upload

    Drag the files into the browser window; or click the Upload button on the right of the top bar and choose the files in the dialog.

  4. Watch the progress

    While uploading, you'll usually see a progress bar or a counter. Large files or a large number of photos take time, so do not rush to close the window.

  5. Wait for it to finish

    When it is done, the photos appear on the timeline. If one is shown as "duplicate" or skipped, the same file was uploaded before.

  6. Spot-check the result

    Open a few photos and check that the image and the capture date look right. If something is off, write it down first, then troubleshoot with Chapter 12.

Do not close the browser mid-upload: the upload runs live, and closing the tab interrupts anything that has not finished. Wait for the progress to complete before you leave.

Duplicate detection: the same photo is never uploaded twice

By default, Immich runs duplicate detection: if an identical file is already on the server, uploading it again is skipped, so no space is wasted. This helps a lot when you drag the same folder in more than once.

Warning: duplicate detection compares file contents (a hash), not whether two photos "look alike". The same photo that has been edited or resized counts as a different file.

Common upload pitfalls

  1. The upload fails partway through

    Check that your network is stable first, then retry. Large video files fail more often, so you can upload them in batches.

  2. The photos do not show up on the timeline

    Check search or your albums first; the capture date may have placed them somewhere in the past. Try the search from Chapter 7.

  3. It says there is not enough storage space

    The server is nearly full. Clean up duplicates and the trash first, or contact your admin (Chapter 10).

  4. Video uploads are slow

    Video files are large and need transcoding to generate thumbnails, so they are slow by nature. Be patient, or switch to background backup from the Immich mobile app in Chapter 9.

Upload FAQ

Which file formats are supported?
The common ones: JPG, PNG, HEIC, GIF and WebP, plus video formats such as MP4 and MOV. Check the official docs for the definitive list.
How many photos can I upload at once?
There is no hard limit on the number of photos, but uploading too many at once makes the upload take longer and uses more memory. Upload in batches; a few hundred per batch is a good size.
Can I delete the original files on my computer after uploading?
Yes, but we recommend you first confirm that every photo uploaded correctly and that the server side is backed up, and only then delete the local originals. Otherwise you could end up with neither copy.
Does it compress my photos?
By default, Immich keeps the original file and generates separate thumbnails and transcoded copies for quick browsing. It never degrades the original quality to save space.
Why is the date wrong on some photos?
Usually the capture time in the original file's EXIF data is missing or was changed. You can check and correct it in the photo's info panel; see Chapter 5.
