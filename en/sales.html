Why your home needs a self-hosted photo library

The photos on a phone are the family's biggest digital asset: children growing up, family gatherings, trips — tens of thousands of them before you notice. Hand them to a cloud service and you pay a monthly fee, the photos sit in someone else's data center, and when the service changes its rules you follow along. Lose or break the phone, and the memories can go with it.

A self-hosted photo library brings the "Google Photos experience" back home: the photos live on your own machine, the interface is just as easy to use, and the difference is that the data is in your hands.

The one-line pitch: "A cloud photo service is a rented safe-deposit box; a self-hosted photo library is one you own. The contents are yours either way — the difference is who holds the key."

Your data stays home, and your privacy stays yours

The core selling point of a self-hosted photo library is control:

  • Photos and videos live on your own server and never pass through a third-party cloud.
  • AI analysis (face recognition, semantic search) also runs on the machine at home, so the photos never leak out.
  • You control the shared links and can revoke them at any time.
Honest caveat: the benefit of self-hosting is control, and the responsibility is yours too. WoowTech looks after three things for you — backups, updates and security — so you get the control without carrying the maintenance burden.

    FAQ

    Will anyone see my photos?
    No. The data sits on your own machine (or on your own dedicated cloud host), and the AI analysis runs locally too, so nothing leaks out.
    How is this different from Google Photos?
    The experience is very similar, but the photos are in your hands, there is no monthly fee, and no service can change the rules on you.
    Do I need to know how to set up a server?
    No. On your side you only open a web page and install the app; WoowTech takes care of deployment and maintenance.
    What if I run out of storage space?
    Storage can be expanded, depending on the option. The cloud PaaS option comes with S3 storage built in, which scales with your usage.
    Do photos from my phone back up automatically?
    Yes. Install the app and turn on automatic backup, and everything you shoot after that lands at home over Wi-Fi.
