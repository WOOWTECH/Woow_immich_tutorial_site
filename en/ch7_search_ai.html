Chapter 7

Search and AI

Find photos by keyword, by person, by place, or even by describing them in one sentence. By the end of this chapter, you'll know how Immich's AI automatically picks out the people in your photos and enables semantic search, so that tens of thousands of photos can be found in an instant.

With many photos, searching beats scrolling

Once you have more than a few thousand photos, "I remember where it is" stops being reliable. Immich's search and AI let you go straight to the photo you want with "beach", "birthday cake", someone's name, or a one-sentence description. It is one of the upgrades you feel most in a self-hosted photo library.

Chapter task: search by keyword, and get to know how people and places are organized. Search is read-only; it never changes your photos.

What AI search does behind the scenes

Immich's AI is made up of a few "smart features". After a photo is uploaded, the server's background jobs analyze it automatically:

FeatureWhat it doesHow you use it
Face recognition (Faces)Finds the faces in your photos and groups the same person under PeopleName a person; after that, click the name to find them
Semantic searchUnderstands what is in the picture, so you can find photos in natural languageSearch for a description like "a dog on the beach"
Object detectionRecognizes the objects and scenes in a photoSearch for "car", "cat", "mountain" and so on
PlacesReads the GPS data and organizes it into placesFind photos from "Okinawa" by place
It takes time: AI analysis is a background job. After a bulk upload, the new photos are not searchable semantically right away; the results are only complete once the jobs have finished (see Chapter 10).

Five steps to run a search

  1. Open Explore, or use the search box in the top bar

    Click Explore in the sidebar, or type into the "Search your photos" box in the top bar.

  2. Type a keyword

    Try "sea", "cake", "cat", or a one-sentence description. The results appear right away.

  3. Find by person

    In the People section of Explore, click a person's thumbnail to see every photo they are in.

  4. Find by place

    Pick a place in the Places section, or click a location on the map (Map).

  5. Combine filters

    Depending on the version, you can combine person + place + keyword to narrow the results and pin down the target quickly.

Name people to make search more useful

Face recognition first groups the same face as an unnamed person; you can give them a name. Once named, that person's photos can be found by name and are easier to organize.

Names are private: a person's name exists only in your photo library and is never made public. Just use a name you'll recognize.

Common search pitfalls

  1. Photos you just uploaded do not show up in search

    AI analysis takes time. Wait for the background jobs to finish, then try again. See Chapter 10 to check the status of the jobs.

  2. Face recognition mixes different people together

    Face recognition occasionally gets it wrong. You can merge or split people by hand on the people page to correct the grouping.

  3. Semantic search results look strange

    AI search is approximate, not exact. Rephrase, or combine a keyword with a place, and the results get more accurate.

  4. Location data is missing

    A photo without GPS data has no place. You can add the location in the info panel.

Search FAQ

Does AI analysis send my photos to the cloud?
No. Immich's AI runs on your own server, so your photos are never sent out to a third party for analysis. That is exactly the advantage of a self-hosted photo library.
Does semantic search work in languages other than English?
It depends on the AI model in use; most models support multiple languages. The actual results depend on your server settings, so test with simple keywords first. Note: the Chinese edition asks about Chinese specifically; the answer is the same for any language.
Can face recognition be turned off?
Yes. An admin turns it on or off in the settings (Chapter 10). Once it is off, faces are no longer recognized automatically.
Can I change a person's name after naming them?
Yes. Go to the people page and click the person to rename or merge them.
Why were some photos not analyzed by the AI?
The jobs may not have reached them yet, or the file format is not supported. Wait for the jobs to finish, or check the troubleshooting in Chapter 12.
