Chapter 2

Login and accounts

Open the Immich entry point in a browser, log in to your account, and get to know the account details you see after login. By the end of this chapter, you'll be able to get in and out of this photo library safely, and you'll know where to look when a login gets stuck.

Logging in is the door to your photos

Immich is a multi-user photo library: everyone has their own account, and you only see your own photos once you are logged in. This "door" protects your memories, and it also decides which features you can use later on. Logging in correctly is the starting point for every chapter that follows.

Logging in is simple in itself; the details are all about security: the URL has to be right, the password has to be kept safe, and you have to sign out when you are done. This chapter covers all of that in one place.

Chapter task: log in once, confirm your own account details, and learn how to sign out. Nothing in this chapter changes any account or server setting.

Where accounts come from and what roles they have

You do not register an Immich account yourself; an admin creates it on the server (see Chapter 10). What you receive is an "email + password" pair.

ItemWhat it meansWatch out
EmailYour unique login identifier, for example you@example.comIt is not for receiving mail; it is mainly the login identifier
PasswordThe login credential you setKeep it safe, and do not write it down anywhere public
RoleAdmin or regular userYour role decides whether you can use the admin features in Chapter 10
Do not reuse passwords: your Immich password should not be the same as the one you use for any other service. If one of them ever leaks, the other accounts do not go down with it.

Five steps to log in

  1. Open a browser and enter the entry URL

    In the address bar, type https://your-immich.example. Note: replace this with the URL of your own Immich server (the one WoowTech gave you, or your own). Make sure it is https, and spell it out in full. Once you see the Immich login screen, you are in the right place.

  2. Enter your email

    Type your account email into the "Email" field. The field usually shows the placeholder text "Email".

  3. Enter your password

    Type your password into the "Password" field. It is shown as dots so that nobody next to you can read it.

  4. Press Login

    Click the "Login" button (or just press Enter). On success you land on the main photos screen, /photos, and the sidebar appears on the left.

  5. Confirm it is your account

    Click the avatar in the top right and check that it shows your name and email. If it does, the login is complete.

Stay signed in: if the login page has an option such as "Remember me", selecting it lets the browser keep you signed in, so you do not have to retype your password for a while. Do not select it on a shared (public) computer.

Your account details after login, and signing out

After login, the avatar in the top right is the entry point to your account. Click it and you'll see:

  • Your account name and email: check that this is your account.
  • Account settings: change your display name, password, appearance and so on (depending on the version).
  • Sign out / Logout: ends your login on this device.
  • An admin-only entry point: if you are an admin, you'll also see "Administration", which opens the admin panel (Chapter 10).
Always sign out on shared computers: when you have finished using a library or a shared office computer, you must sign out from the avatar menu and close the browser. Otherwise the next person may walk straight into your photo library.

Common login pitfalls

  1. The URL will not open

    Check that the URL is complete and correct, and that your network is working. If the browser says "cannot connect" or "Not secure", stop there: it may be a network or certificate problem, so contact your admin.

  2. Wrong password

    Check the capitalization, whether Caps Lock or a different keyboard layout/input method is on, and whether there is a stray space at the start or end. Too many failed attempts in a row may lock you out temporarily; wait a little and try again.

  3. Forgot your password

    Immich has no self-service reset; ask your admin to reset your password in the admin panel (Chapter 10). Do not try to guess it yourself.

  4. The screen is blank or keeps spinning after login

    Refresh once. If that does not help, the server may be busy or updating. Wait a few minutes, or try from the app (Chapter 9).

Login FAQ

Why don't I have an account, and how do I get one?
Immich accounts are created by an admin; you cannot register one yourself. When you need an account, give the admin the email you want to use, and the admin creates it in the admin panel and gives you an initial password.
Can I log in to the same account on my phone as well?
Yes. The same account can be logged in from a browser and the Immich mobile app at the same time. Chapter 9 explains how to set up the mobile app.
Should I select "Remember me"?
On your own private device, yes; it saves you retyping the password every time. On a public or shared device, no; and remember to sign out when you are done.
Do my photos disappear when I sign out?
No. Signing out only ends your login on this device. The photos stay on the server, and you'll see them again the next time you log in.
Can I change my own password?
Usually yes, under the avatar in the top right → Account settings. If you cannot find it, ask your admin to reset it.
